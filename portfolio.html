01 / Software

Playnck

A powerful local music player, built from the ground up.

What it is

Playnck is a desktop music player built with Electron, designed around a clean interface and a focused listening experience. It manages a local music library, with playlists, metadata and album artwork, and it covers the jobs that usually need separate tools: tagging, conversion and playback.

I took it from a blank Electron project to a packaged, installable Windows app, with releases published through GitHub.

Download latest version View on GitHub
Library
Songs, albums, artists, genres, folders, playlists and listening history, with a home view of library totals.
Tagging
Edit metadata and cover art. Identify tracks from their audio through AcoustID and MusicBrainz, or search by title and artist.
Playback
Ten band equalizer with presets, gapless playback, synced lyrics with timing adjustment, sleep timer and keyboard shortcuts.
Conversion
A conversion queue for audio files, powered by FFmpeg.
Personalization
Background and accent colors, language, and backup and restore.
Built with
Electron, Node.js, JavaScript, HTML and CSS. Packaged with Electron Builder and NSIS.
02 / Research

FANET simulation with NS-3

Routing protocols and mobility models for drone networks. Master's thesis, 2024.

A Flying Ad-hoc Network is a group of UAVs that communicate directly with one another and with the ground, without fixed infrastructure. Nodes move quickly, links appear and disappear, and routing has to keep up.

For my thesis at Université Ferhat Abbas Sétif 1, I used NS-3 to model and simulate UAV communication, mobility and routing scenarios. The work compares the AODV, OLSR, DSDV and DSR routing protocols and analyzes how different mobility models affect the performance of the network.
