Background

My foundation is in Computer Science, with a particular pull toward Networks & Distributed Systems — the problems that show up when many independent parts have to agree, coordinate, or simply stay in touch. Alongside that, I build software end to end, and maintain an active research interest in simulation-based networking work.

Photography developed in parallel, not as a break from the technical work but as another way of practicing the same underlying habit: paying close, structured attention to something and deciding what matters.
