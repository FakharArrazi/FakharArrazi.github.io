About

Aidoudi El Fakhar Arrazi.

A Master's in Networks & Distributed Systems, practical experience building software end to end, and a parallel, equally serious practice in photography and design. Currently looking for a junior role in software development, systems, or networking.

Background

My foundation is in Computer Science, with a Master's specialization in Networks & Distributed Systems — specifically Flying Ad-hoc Networks (FANET) and simulation under NS-3. That research interest sits alongside practical software development: I designed and built Playnck, a desktop music player, from a blank Electron project to a packaged, installable Windows app with real users in mind, not just a class exercise.

Outside the technical work, I design and shoot — graphic work in Photoshop and Illustrator, and photography and video across portraits, products, and events. I treat both sides as one practice rather than a day job and a hobby: the same attention to structure, detail, and what's worth keeping shows up whether I'm debugging a routing protocol or laying out a poster.
