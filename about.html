I am a computer scientist who builds software, researches how networks behave, and works with a camera and design tools. People expect those to be separate careers. For me they are one way of working.

Foundations

I studied computer systems at Université Ferhat Abbas Sétif 1, finishing the Licence in 2022 with a sales management application for a supermarket, then specialized in Networks and Distributed Systems for my Master's, completed in 2024.

Research

My thesis simulated applications for drone networks. Using NS-3, I modeled Flying Ad-hoc Networks, compared the AODV, OLSR, DSDV and DSR routing protocols, and analyzed how mobility models change network performance.

See the research

Building

Alongside the research I designed and built Playnck, a desktop music player, from a blank Electron project to a packaged Windows installer. It handles a local library, automatic tagging through AcoustID and MusicBrainz, and audio conversion with FFmpeg.

See the project

Images

I have also worked professionally with images. At DZ Production I designed print and digital materials, shot photography and video for portraits, products, weddings and events, and handled DTF printing. The same habits carry across: structure first, then detail, then deciding what to leave out. It applies whether I am debugging a routing protocol or laying out a poster.
