Software is where I build. Networks are where I investigate. Photography is where I observe.

I move between two disciplines that don't usually share a desk: the precise, distributed logic of computer networks, and the slower, more deliberate eye of photography. One is about how systems move information across distance; the other is about what's worth holding still.

My work spans software development, research into networked and distributed systems, and an ongoing photography practice — treated here as equally serious parts of the same person, not a developer's site with a hobby bolted on.

What I do

  • 01 Software Building applications end to end, from interface details to the systems underneath them.
  • 02 Networks & Distributed Systems Studying how independent nodes coordinate, fail, and recover — with a particular interest in mobile and ad-hoc networks.
  • 03 Research Simulation-driven exploration of networking problems, including work with Flying Ad-hoc Networks and NS-3.
  • 04 Photography An ongoing visual practice across street, landscape and portrait work — composition as its own discipline.
