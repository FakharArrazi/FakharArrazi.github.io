Selected work, 01

Playnck

A desktop music player for local libraries, built with Electron.

Playnck manages and plays a local music library. It identifies tracks from their audio, edits tags and cover art, converts files with FFmpeg and ships as a Windows installer. It was built from an empty project to a packaged release.

  • Automatic tagging with AcoustID and MusicBrainz
  • Audio conversion with FFmpeg
  • Ten band equalizer and synced lyrics
  • Playlists, folders, history and themes

Selected work, 02

Routing and mobility in drone networks

My Master's thesis simulated applications for Flying Ad-hoc Networks (FANET) in NS-3. I modeled UAV communication, compared four routing protocols and analyzed how mobility models affect the performance of drone networks.

NS-3
Protocols
